# Lesson 07: git log - Reading Your Commit History

## What this lesson covers

Navigating and making sense of a project's history.

### Intuition to build
- `git log` is your project's journal - every decision is recorded
- SHA hashes are just unique IDs for commits - not scary, just fingerprints
- HEAD is a pointer to wherever you currently are

### What to include
- `git log` default output - reading the SHA, author, date, message
- `git log --oneline` - the format you'll use 90% of the time
- `git log --oneline --graph --all` - visualising branches
- What a SHA hash is and why it's unique
- Introducing HEAD - "you are here"
- `git show <SHA>` - inspect a specific commit
